# Shared Project Brain — Business Rules

## Financial Rules
1. **Net Balance**: `Net Balance = Total Approved Income + Sunday Offerings - Total Approved Expenses`.
2. **Fund Balance**: `Fund Balance = Opening Balance + Fund Incomes + Fund Offerings - Fund Expenses`.
3. **Approval Thresholds**:
   - Expenses > ฿10,000 require Treasurer or Admin approval.
   - Pending expenses do not deduct from available balance until status is set to `approved`.
4. **Offerings**:
   - Sunday Count Sheets require two-person verification before posting.
   - Tithes linked to members generate annual tax deduction receipts (Tax Receipts).
