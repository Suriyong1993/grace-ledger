# Shared Project Brain — Product Vision

## Long-term Vision

To provide local churches with a modern, secure, and beautiful financial operating system that eliminates administrative friction, ensures total audit transparency, and honors every contribution with digital stewardship.

## Principles

- **No AI Slop**: Clean, bespoke financial UI with high data density and editorial typography.
- **Immutable Auditability**: All financial entries are tracked with timestamped actor logs and cryptographic verifications.
- **Zero Ambiguity**: Clear business rules for fund balances, offering categories, and budget constraints.
