Redesign Master Plan

Built on the verified facts in the Strategic Review. Scope agreed: evolve the identity (same palette family, Sarabun + Inter, fixed finance colors), redesign hierarchy, IA, workflows and components. Concepts are mocked in Redesign Concepts. No code modified.

Executive summary

Grace Ledger's screens are individually competent and collectively page-shaped, not work-shaped. Every route opens with a big number and cards; none opens with the thing the user came to do. The weekly job (count Sunday cash with two people, resolve a variance, post, deposit Monday) is buried three clicks deep behind a dashboard that repeats the Funds page. Approvals — the highest-stakes decision — render the detail inline under the queue with no focus change. Amounts are excellent (tabular Inter, sign + color, decimal.js); hierarchy around them is not.

Vision: a calm financial workbench where the home screen is this week's work, every workflow has a visible lifecycle, every decision shows its fund impact before the button, and the visual system uses one card grammar, one radius family (16 / 12), one accent, and borders — not tint — for structure. Direction: Concept B (Guided Workflow) as the spine, with A's density in Transactions/Reports and C's fund-health table in Funds.

Recommended direction

B as spine · A's density where the job is lookup · C's fund-health table where the job is oversight.

  • Why B: the product's own audit (M3_PRODUCT_AUDIT) names treasurer + 2 counters as primary users, weekly frequency, cash-loss as the risk. B is the only concept whose home screen serves that. It also fixes the two open PREMIUM findings (P1-10 detail-not-a-sheet, P1-8 dates) as part of the flow, not as patches.
  • Why not pure A: CLAUDE.md audience is "not accounting experts"; a ledger-first home optimises for the wrong person and fails on the device counters actually hold.
  • Why not pure C: beautiful monthly, useless on Sunday. Its fund-health table is the right Funds page, not the home.
  • Consistency: all three share the shell, tokens and card grammar — mixing them is a page-level choice, not a visual fork.

New design language (evolution, not replacement)

Personality → decisions

CalmTwo backgrounds only (off-white page, white surface). Tint is reserved for state (variance red, pending amber) — never for KPI tiles or table headers (fixes Reports).
ExactEvery amount: .num-display, sign + color, 2 decimals, right-aligned in tables. ฿ in its own span at normal tracking (PREMIUM P1-1). One date format: 23 ส.ค. 2569, native date inputs replaced by a formatted trigger.
StructuredBorders carry structure. Lists are ledger rows inside one bordered surface, not stacks of cards; KPI groups are border-joined strips (Concept A) not four floating cards. Cards-in-cards banned.
GuidedEvery multi-step workflow renders the same lifecycle stepper; the current step is the only place amber appears; exactly one orange button per screen.
HonestLabels match the query (no "เดือนนี้" on an all-time sum); no fixed chart scales; empty states say the one true thing + next action.

Color

Unchanged palette. Rules tightened: --primary = the one CTA + active nav + focus ring, never a number, never a bar (fixes Funds/Members). Budget progress bars use --foreground. --info (blue) is retired from the UI except the "posted" badge (D2). Surface: --card; elevated surface: --card + --shadow-elevated, max one per screen. Text: --foreground; muted: --muted-foreground; on-tint text: --on-*-muted.

Typography

Page title--text-2xl 22px (desktop) / --text-xl (mobile), display 600, −0.02em. Down from 26–40px: titles were competing with money.
Section heading--text-md 16px, 600
Kicker.kicker 11px caps — the universal section label
Body / row title--text-base 15px 500 / meta --text-xs 12px muted
Hero amount--text-4xl 32px 700 (was 40–60px); only one per screen
Table numerals--text-sm 13px, tabular, right-aligned, 600 for totals
Off-scale sizesBanned (lint).

Spacing · layout · elevation

  • 4px scale unchanged. Page padding 24/32; section gap 28; row padding 14 × 24; table cell 10 × 14.
  • Shell: sidebar 240 (desktop), bottom nav (≤768) — unchanged. Content max 1180. Right sheet 480px for details (approvals, transaction, create) — new primitive replacing centered modals for anything with more than one field.
  • Radius family (post-D1): surfaces 16, controls 12, pills full, tables 0. Shadow: border by default; --shadow-elevated on sheets/dialogs and the one hero surface.
  • Motion: unchanged tokens; sheet slide 220ms ease-out; nothing else animates.

Technical migration strategy

No framework. The redesign is expressed entirely as: (1) token value updates, (2) app.css classes, (3) string-returning helpers in src/components/shared/, (4) render-string edits in pages, (5) one new routing-visible state (approval sheet) handled by the existing /approvals/:id pattern. Full re-render remains; the sheet is re-rendered open from route state, so no transition state is lost. Focus management is done in attachEventListeners after render (already the pattern in RejectionModal).
